# Root-Cause Hypotheses & Idea Clusters

1. Core issue (cause): the yard runs on fixed rules with incomplete data. Blocks are not zoned by demand, stacks ignore pickup order and exact position, the layout never adapts as demand shifts by season, and release status is not shared.
2. Wrong place: busy container groups sit far from the gate, rail and quay exits, so every move travels further (32 minutes inside).
3. Wrong order: stacks are built without knowing pickup time or exact slot, so cranes reshuffle boxes to dig out the one needed (rehandles and $6M overtime).
4. Wrong time: demand shifts (a product peaking in December but not in March) while the layout stays fixed, and customers learn late that boxes are released (6-day dwell, 40-minute queues).
5. Symptoms, not causes: yard crowding, $6M overtime, $8M penalties, customers unable to plan and staff anxiety.

Clusters: (A) operate the existing system better, (B) targeted retrofit of the constraint, (C) new capacity / architecture.
