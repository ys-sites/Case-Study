# Root-Cause Hypotheses & Idea Clusters

1. Core issue (cause): fragmented release and schedule information. Release status, vessel changes and pickup plans are not shared across teams, truckers, rail or customers, and planning runs in separate systems on fixed rules.
2. It drives dwell: customers learn late that a container is released, so pickups slip and boxes sit in the yard.
3. It drives truck queues: with no appointments or readiness information, trucks arrive in concentrated windows and queue 40 minutes.
4. It drives rehandles: the system knows the stack but not the exact position, and stacks are not built around known pickup times, so crane time goes on reshuffles and trucks spend 32 minutes inside.
5. Symptoms, not causes: yard crowding, $6M overtime, $8M penalties, customers unable to plan and staff anxiety. More land or more overtime treats these without removing the cause.

Clusters: (A) operate the existing system better, (B) targeted retrofit of the constraint, (C) new capacity / architecture.
