# How to Tackle

**1. How to tackle this case**
How to tackle case ENG1M-0000001. A step-by-step method you can repeat on any case like this one.

**2. The path winning teams follow**
Cases like this follow the operations & engineering improvement model: Baseline KPIs, then bottleneck/root cause, then optimize/simulate, then pilot, then ROI + KPIs.

**3. Break the question into three MECE branches**
Start with one question: How do we achieve 8.5% capacity scaling improvement within 3 months and 866 thousand dollars? Split it into three branches with no overlap: Demand & mix, Capacity at the constraint, Flow between stages. Each person on the team owns one branch.

**4. Commit to a guess in the first 20 minutes**
Within twenty minutes, write down a hypothesis you can prove wrong. Here: The gap is one binding constraint, not total capacity: relieving it delivers the target without rebuilding the whole system. If the data kills it, pivot and say so. Judges reward honesty.

**5. The tools that fit this problem**
Use only the tools that fit. Theory of Constraints (5 focusing steps). OEE = Availability × Performance × Quality. Little's Law: WIP = Throughput × Flow time. Then do the quick math: Gap: 1,190.00 , then 1,291.15 units/day. Budget per % point of target: 102 thousand dollars.

**6. Compare every option on the same basis**
Score all three options with explicit weights: target attainment, return, payback, downside resilience and budget headroom. The budget is a hard gate: an option over budget cannot win.

**7. Look at four levels before you choose**
Then build a solution ladder. Level one, the quick win. Level two, the cheapest option that meets the target. Level three, the value maximiser. Level four, the stretch. Your recommendation should explain where it sits on this ladder.

**8. Five hours, five blocks**
Finally, protect your time. 0:00 to 0:40, Read & align. 0:40 to 1:50, Data & quick math. 1:50 to 3:20, Options & decision. 3:20 to 4:20, Deck. 4:20 to 5:00, Rehearse & Q&A. Video three shows the answer this method produces.
