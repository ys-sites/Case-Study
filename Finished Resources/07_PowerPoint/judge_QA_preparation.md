# Judge Q&A Preparation

### [Stress test] What changes when unexpected demand increase persists and the selected design underperforms by 15%?
**A:** Bear case (performance -15%, capex +20%): 11.0% improvement, NPV CAD $1,996,871, payback 14.9 months. Target is still met.

### [Alternatives] Why not Option A, the cheapest?
**A:** It costs CAD $232,000 but delivers 4.6% (misses the 8.5% target); it scores 4.6/10 vs 8.55/10 for Option C.

### [Assumptions] How confident are you in the option estimates?
**A:** Source: case datum. We treat them as hypotheses: the Horizon 1 pilot must reach 80% of the modelled gain before scale-up spend.

### [Financials] What does it cost to run every year?
**A:** O&M of CAD $33,800/yr is already netted out of the CAD $947,537/yr benefit.

### [Financials] What is the benefit-to-cost ratio?
**A:** 4.33 on a present-value basis at 9.5% WACC; levelized 5.43 CAD per added unit.

### [Implementation] What if implementation takes 50% longer?
**A:** NPV falls to CAD $2,630,575; the H1 pilot and fixed-price packages protect the schedule.

### [Implementation] How will you know it is working by day 30?
**A:** Pilot KPI: Pilot reaches >= 2.5% improvement; data quality verified. Leading indicators: Constraint utilisation, OEE at the constraint.

### [Safety] What are the safety and regulatory implications?
**A:** Every change goes through management-of-change review and HAZOP before commissioning; designs are validated against local codes and vendor specifications, with licensed engineering review before deployment.

### [Upside] What would you do with 20% more budget?
**A:** Accelerate the rollout and add redundancy at the constraint.
