# Judge Q&A Preparation

### [Stress test] Why not just build on the 15-acre parcel?
**A:** Space alone fails the objective: 25,344 / 28,000 TEU = 90.5% at year 5, for about $14.5M, over budget. Cutting dwell to 4.7 days reaches about 83% on today's footprint.

### [Stress test] Why only two AGVs?
**A:** The evidence is mixed: successful automated terminals cut operating costs 25-55%, but some lose 7-15% productivity. Two units in a fenced lane prove uptime, cost per move and safety for $1.6M; the fleet grows only if the month-12 gate passes.

### [Stress test] How do the MIP and CRAFT work together?
**A:** The MIP assigns container groups to blocks, minimising move frequency x distance to their exit within capacity and segregation rules. CRAFT then tries pairwise zone swaps and keeps any that lower total flow x distance, so the layout can be re-improved as demand shifts.

### [Stress test] What if the seasonal forecast is wrong?
**A:** Slotting re-runs weekly on actual bookings, not only the forecast; planners approve every re-slot; zoning is reviewed monthly.

### [Stress test] How do you know where every container is?
**A:** OCR cameras read container numbers at the gate and quay, crane position sensors record the exact slot of every pick and drop, and RFID tags identify each AGV and truck. Every move writes to one yard database that the slotting engine, the AGVs and the customer portal all read.

### [Stress test] Will automation cost jobs, and who maintains the AGVs?
**A:** No layoffs from this program. Drivers can retrain as AGV supervisors and technicians. AGVs follow a preventive maintenance plan with a vendor SLA, and manual tractors stay as the fallback.

### [Alternatives] Why not Option A, the cheapest?
**A:** It costs CAD $1,600,000 but delivers 3.9% (misses the 19.5% target); it scores 5.22/10 vs 6.92/10 for Option B.

### [Assumptions] How confident are you in the option estimates?
**A:** Source: assumption: estimate (overtime -40%, penalties -50%; dwell 6.0 to 4.7 days). We treat them as hypotheses: the Horizon 1 pilot must reach 80% of the modelled gain before scale-up spend.

### [Financials] What does it cost to run every year?
**A:** O&M of CAD $1,300,000/yr is already netted out of the CAD $6,400,000/yr benefit.

### [Financials] What is the benefit-to-cost ratio?
**A:** 1.42 on a present-value basis at 9.5% WACC; levelized 134474.76 CAD per year per % point of improvement.

### [Implementation] What if implementation takes 50% longer?
**A:** NPV falls to CAD $4,223,006; the H1 pilot and fixed-price packages protect the schedule.

### [Implementation] How will you know it is working by day 30?
**A:** Pilot KPI: Move data cleaned; MIP/CRAFT layout validated in simulation; AGV vendor and safety case approved. Leading indicators: Constraint utilisation, OEE at the constraint.

### [Safety] What are the safety and regulatory implications?
**A:** Every change goes through management-of-change review and HAZOP before commissioning; designs are validated against local codes and vendor specifications, with licensed engineering review before deployment.

### [Upside] What would you do with 20% more budget?
**A:** Accelerate the rollout and add redundancy at the constraint.
