# Judge Q&A Preparation

### [Stress test] Why not just build on the 15-acre parcel?
**A:** Space alone fails the objective: 25,344 TEU / 28,000 TEU = 90.5% at year 5. It costs about $14.5M, over the $10M budget, and must be phased around live operations. Cutting dwell to 4.7 days reaches about 83% on today's footprint. We keep the parcel as a phase-2 option if dwell stays above 5.6 days.

### [Stress test] What if dwell does not fall as much as you assume?
**A:** The threshold is explicit: dwell at or below 4.83 days meets 85% without land. If dwell is above 5.3 days at month 9 (our gate trigger), we tighten the free-time tariff and start the parcel design. With dwell at 5.6 days or less, the parcel would then meet the objective.

### [Stress test] How confident are you in the $6.4M of annual savings?
**A:** Each saving has an owner and a mechanism. Break-even needs only $3.1M a year. If penalties fall 25% instead of 50%, the five-year impact is still -$6.5M.

### [Stress test] Will the AI and automation cost jobs?
**A:** No layoffs from this program. The AI recommends and planners decide. Roles shift from manual re-planning and overtime to planning-cell and exception work, with training in paid time.

### [Stress test] How do you keep the terminal running during the rollout?
**A:** Lane-by-lane appointments with a walk-in lane kept open, block-by-block positioning, 6 weeks of shadow mode for the AI, go-lives outside vessel peaks, and a rollback plan triggered if gate throughput drops below baseline for 2 shifts.

### [Stress test] How will you get truckers to use appointments?
**A:** We co-design slot rules with them, guarantee the container is ready when a slot is booked, give on-time trucks a priority lane, and keep grace windows. Slots exist only for released containers, so a booking is never wasted.

### [Alternatives] Why not Option A, the cheapest?
**A:** It costs CAD $4,100,000 but delivers 10% (misses the 19.5% target); it scores 5.63/10 vs 7.0/10 for Option B.

### [Assumptions] How confident are you in the option estimates?
**A:** Source: assumption: estimate (overtime -40%, penalties -50%; dwell 6.0 to 4.7 days). We treat them as hypotheses: the Horizon 1 pilot must reach 80% of the modelled gain before scale-up spend.

### [Financials] What does it cost to run every year?
**A:** O&M of CAD $1,300,000/yr is already netted out of the CAD $6,400,000/yr benefit.

### [Financials] What is the benefit-to-cost ratio?
**A:** 1.46 on a present-value basis at 9.5% WACC; levelized 128752.43 CAD per year per % point of improvement.

### [Implementation] What if implementation takes 50% longer?
**A:** NPV falls to CAD $4,623,006; the H1 pilot and fixed-price packages protect the schedule.

### [Implementation] How will you know it is working by day 30?
**A:** Pilot KPI: Pilot lanes: queue time -25%; release status shared within 1 hour; baseline rehandles measured. Leading indicators: Constraint utilisation, OEE at the constraint.

### [Safety] What are the safety and regulatory implications?
**A:** Every change goes through management-of-change review and HAZOP before commissioning; designs are validated against local codes and vendor specifications, with licensed engineering review before deployment.

### [Upside] What would you do with 20% more budget?
**A:** Accelerate the rollout and add redundancy at the constraint.
