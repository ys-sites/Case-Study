# Problem Statement

Which package, within the $10M upfront budget, attacks PortFlow's core issue so that at +20% volume the yard runs at or below 85% utilization and trucks turn in under 45 minutes by year five?

The yard is static and blind. Today 21,120 of 24,000 TEU are in use (88%), containers stay 6 days and trucks take 72 minutes. With +20% volume the yard passes its capacity by year 5 unless containers are placed, moved and released faster; the 15-acre parcel alone would still leave about 90%.
