# Problem Statement

Which package, within the $10M upfront budget, attacks PortFlow's core issue so that at +20% volume the yard runs at or below 85% utilization and trucks turn in under 45 minutes by year five?

The yard problem is a dwell-time problem, not a space problem. Little's Law: 3,520 TEU/day x 6 days = 21,120 TEU (88% of 24,000). At +20% volume (4,224 TEU/day) the same 6-day dwell needs 25,344 TEU, 106% of capacity. Even with the 15-acre parcel (28,000 TEU) utilization would be 90.5%, so space alone cannot meet the 85% objective. Dwell must fall to 4.83 days or less (-19.5%).
