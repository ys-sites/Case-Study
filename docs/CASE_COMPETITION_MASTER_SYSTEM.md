## 1. THE 5-STAGE CASE COMPETITION ARCHITECTURE

Every case analysis, strategy memo, presentation deck, or autonomous simulation must conform to this 5-stage standard:

### Stage 1: Executive Summary & MECE Problem Diagnosis
- **The Hook (First 60 Seconds)**: Deliver an immediate, decisive executive recommendation ("The Big Idea"). Avoid reciting case background to judges who already know it.
- **MECE Problem Framing**: Separate root causes from surface symptoms using Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive issue trees.
- **Quantified Baseline**: Frame the bottleneck with tangible baseline numbers (e.g., *Current output: 1,042 units/day; Downtime variance: +/- 18%*).

### Stage 2: Strategic Formulas & Theoretical Frameworks
Retain and state all business and financial formulas explicitly with defined variables, units, and benchmarks:
- **Market Triangulation**: $\text{TAM} \longrightarrow \text{SAM} \longrightarrow \text{SOM}$
- **Unit Economics**: $\text{LTV} = \frac{\text{ARPU} \times \text{Gross Margin}}{\text{Churn Rate}}$ and $\frac{\text{LTV}}{\text{CAC}} \ge 3.0\times$
- **Breakeven Volume**: $\text{BEP} = \frac{\text{Fixed Implementation Costs}}{\text{Unit Contribution Margin}}$
- **Capital Hurdle**: $\text{WACC}$, $\text{NPV}$, $\text{IRR}$, and Payback Horizon ($\le 12 \text{ months}$).

### Stage 3: Step-by-Step Financial Proofs & Proformas
- **Zero Skipped Math**: Include intermediate arithmetic ($11 + 7 = 18$).
- **3-Year Proforma P&L**: Full breakdown of Gross Revenue, COGS, Gross Margin, R&D, Sales/Marketing, G&A, and EBITDA.
- **3-Scenario Sensitivity**:
  - *Bear Case (-20% Demand / +15% Costs)*: Validates liquidity floor.
  - *Base Case (Approved Plan)*: Core operational target.
  - *Bull Case (+25% Adoption / -10% Churn)*: Upside potential.

### Stage 4: 3-Horizon Roadmap & Risk Pre-Mortem
- **Horizon 1 (Months 1–3)**: Foundation, pilot deployment, telemetry baseline.
- **Horizon 2 (Months 4–8)**: Commercial expansion, facility-wide rollout, process integration.
- **Horizon 3 (Months 9–12)**: Mature operations, optimization, industry standardization.
- **Governance KPIs**: Separate Predictive Leading Indicators (uptime %, pilot adoption) from Financial Lagging Indicators (EBITDA, ROI).
- **2x2 Risk Pre-Mortem**: High Impact / High Probability risks paired with operational mitigations.

### Stage 5: Judge Q&A Stress-Test Defense Bank
- Dedicated bank anticipating the top adversarial judge questions (assumptions sensitivity, competitor retaliation, capital constraints, operational bottlenecks, vendor lead times).
- Evidence-backed rebuttals prepared for rapid presentation defense.

## 2. THE 10-SLIDE MASTER PRESENTATION DECK BLUEPRINT

```text
Slide 01: Executive Summary & Recommendation (The Hook + The Big Idea)
Slide 02: Industry Dynamics & Root-Cause Diagnosis (MECE Issue Tree)
Slide 03: Market Sizing & Segment Focus (TAM ──► SAM ──► SOM)
Slide 04: Strategic Alternatives Evaluation (Why Option B Beats A & C)
Slide 05: The Recommended Strategy (3 Mutually Reinforcing Strategic Pillars)
Slide 06: Operational Go-To-Market & System Architecture
Slide 07: Financial Proforma & Unit Economics (3-Year P&L, Payback, Sensitivity)
Slide 08: Phased Implementation Timeline (Horizon 1-3 Gantt Milestones)
Slide 09: Risk Governance & Pre-Mortem Matrix (2x2 Impact vs. Probability)
Slide 10: Conclusion & Call to Action (Expected ROI, Next 30-Day Priorities)
```

### The Action Title Rule
Every slide header must be a complete declarative sentence communicating the slide's core insight, not a generic noun label:
- *Weak*: "Market Analysis"
- *Strong*: "Mid-Market Industrial Facilities Suffer 18% Annual Margin Erosion Due to Unmonitored Downtime."

## 3. QUANTITATIVE, FINANCIAL & UNIT ECONOMICS FORMULAS

### Customer Unit Economics
$$\text{LTV} = \frac{\text{ARPU} \times \text{Gross Margin \%}}{\text{Annual Churn Rate}}$$
$$\text{LTV / CAC Ratio} \ge 3.0\times \quad (\text{Target: } 3.5\times - 4.5\times)$$
$$\text{Payback Horizon (Months)} = \frac{\text{CAC}}{\text{Monthly Gross Profit per Account}} \le 12 \text{ Months}$$

### Valuation & Investment Hurdle Rates
$$\text{Net Present Value (NPV)} = \sum_{t=1}^{n} \frac{\text{CF}_t}{(1 + \text{WACC})^t} - \text{Initial CapEx}$$
$$\text{Internal Rate of Return (IRR)}: \quad \text{NPV} = 0 \quad (\text{Hurdle: } \text{IRR} \ge 25\%)$$
$$\text{Breakeven Point (Units)} = \frac{\text{Fixed Implementation Overhead}}{\text{Unit Contribution Margin}}$$

## 4. THE 100-POINT COMPETITION JUDGING RUBRIC

| Category | Weight | Target Criteria | High-Score Markers | Deductions |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Strategic Insight & Diagnosis** | **25%** | Problem identification, root-cause depth, MECE issue tree logic. | Isolates core bottleneck; avoids treating symptoms; presents an innovative thesis. | Surface repetition of case text; descriptive rather than diagnostic. |
| **2. Financial Viability & Modeling** | **25%** | 3-year proforma P&L, unit economics, CapEx/OpEx, sensitivity. | Explicit intermediate math; realistic margin progression; 3-scenario matrix. | Arbitrary revenue spikes; omitted CapEx; arithmetic errors. |
| **3. Implementation & Operations** | **20%** | Phased execution roadmap (H1–H3), leading/lagging KPIs. | Clear milestone dates; realistic pilot; organizational ownership. | "Launch nationwide Month 1"; zero discussion of operational bottlenecks. |
| **4. Risk Management & Pre-Mortem** | **15%** | External & internal risk identification, contingency mitigations. | 2x2 matrix with high-impact risks pre-mitigated; defensible pre-mortem plans. | Generic risk list; no actionable contingency plan; ignoring regulations. |
| **5. Executive Presence & Q&A Defense** | **15%** | Storytelling flow, action titles, time management, Q&A resilience. | Slides tell cohesive narrative via action titles alone; air-tight Q&A data defense. | Wall of text; evasive in Q&A; running over the time limit. |

### Scoring Tiers:
- **90–100 (Winner Tier)**: McKinsey/BCG partner-level rigor, airtight numbers, unshakeable Q&A.
- **80–89 (Finalist Tier)**: Sound strategic and financial analysis with minor unit economic gaps.
- **70–79 (Competitor Tier)**: Identifiable strategy but lacks quantitative depth.
- **< 70 (Elimination Tier)**: Critical arithmetic errors, generic recommendations, or surface diagnosis.

## 5. THE 3-HOUR RAPID SPRINT PROTOCOL

For time-boxed competitions, the team operates across 4 synchronized workstreams:

```text
Hour 1: 00:00 - 01:00 │ Deep Reading, MECE Issue Tree, Stated Constraints, Big Idea Hook
Hour 2: 01:00 - 02:00 │ 3-Year Financial Model, Unit Economics ($), 3-Horizon Gantt Roadmap
Hour 3: 02:00 - 02:40 │ 10-Slide Deck Construction, Action Titles, 2x2 Risk Pre-Mortem
Finish: 02:40 - 03:00 │ Pitch Rehearsal, Adversarial Judge Q&A Drill, PDF & PPTX Export
```

## 6. AI CASE COMPETITION ADVISOR & SIMULATION PROTOCOL

When interactive case simulation is activated:
1. Allow the user to select any case ID (`ENG-0001` through `ENG-1000` from `data/engineering_1000_case_simulations.json`) or drop in an uploaded business case.
2. Interactively role-play as client executives, engineers, and board members; disclose additional plausible synthetic data upon specific inquiry.
3. Require a decisive recommendation, at least 3 alternatives, a quantitative financial model, safety analysis, sensitivity tests, and a 10-slide proposal.
4. Score using the 100-point rubric, ask 4 adversarial judge questions, provide feedback, and present the model solution.

## 11. STRICT ANONYMITY, PRIVACY & AUTOMATIC GIT PUSH LAW

1. **Strict Anonymity**: Under no circumstances should any personal name or machine-specific local drive paths (`/path/to/...`, `file:///...`) appear in ANY generated case brief, Markdown strategy guide, review sheet, PDF, HTML, or website file. Use generic designations only (`Strategy Consulting Hub`, `Executive Case Simulation Team`).
2. **Mandatory Automatic Git Push**: On every turn where changes are made, proactively stage (`git add .`), commit with a descriptive message, and push directly to `origin/main` (`https://github.com/ys-sites/Case-Study.git`).
