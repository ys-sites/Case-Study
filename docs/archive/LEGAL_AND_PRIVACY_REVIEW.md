# Québec and Canada: rights and privacy review

Review date: 1 November 2026. This is an engineering and evidence checklist, not a legal opinion or a certification of compliance. The operator, legal status, jurisdiction, actual hosting configuration, retention periods, rights grants and intended audience must be confirmed with qualified Québec counsel and the relevant rights holders.

## Verified implementation and open decisions

| Area | Evidence and action | Open question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| University name and marks | The site calls itself an independent student resource, but uses the university name in its identity. [Concordia's Secretariat](https://www.concordia.ca/about/administration-governance/secretariat.html) states that use of the university name, logo or insignia requires prior permission. | Establish whether permission exists and seek advice or written permission for the actual name, domain and visual identity. The disclaimer is not permission. |
| Course assets and questions | The build uses `website/build/publishedFiles.ts` and `website/src/data/localOnly.ts` to exclude many teacher materials and exam documents. A local post-build scan found 442 files in `dist/courses`, including 93 whose names match broad terms such as lecture, teacher or exam; none of the PDFs matched the narrower teacher/textbook/professor/syllabus/assignment filename pattern. The build reported syncing 783 course files; this discrepancy and separately hosted CDN media need investigation. A filename scan cannot determine ownership, content or legality. | Review each actual public file, question and CDN object, including previously uploaded objects. Confirm creator, rights, permitted use, attribution and public distribution. Written permission or an applicable license may be needed; remove or restrict items after identifying their rights status. The local working inventory is `.work/public-asset-rights-inventory.json`, not a permission ledger. |
| Teacher content | [Concordia Library](https://library.concordia.ca/copyright/overview.html) warns students against distributing course materials without the applicable rights. [CIPO](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canadian-intellectual-property-office/en/copyright-infringement) describes fair dealing as an exception; an educational purpose alone is not automatic clearance. | Verify past-paper questions, teacher-created diagrams, transformed examples, media and derivatives individually. Keep a private provenance ledger. Do not assume a citation, public URL or AI rewrite grants permission. |
| Optional analytics | The app now defaults optional Vercel analytics, Speed Insights and the device-based visitor counter off. A footer control enables them; revoking the choice reloads the page. Historical visitor totals include visits from before this change. | Counsel must assess whether consent and wording are appropriate, including the counter, third-party processing, retention, cross-border processing, deletion and any cached data. Do not claim legal compliance from the toggle alone. |
| Data flows | Progress and study notes use browser storage. The contact form sends name, email and message to FormSubmit; media playback can contact Google, while hosting and security services receive network requests. The visitor counter uses an external counter service. | Confirm real provider accounts, current configurations, IP and request-log processing, locations, retention and contractual terms. The short footer disclosure is not a complete policy. |
| Québec privacy | The [Commission d’accès à l’information](https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca/protection-renseignements-personnels/sujets-et-domaines-dinteret/principaux-changements-loi-25) describes notice, privacy-by-default and privacy officer obligations for applicable organizations, and an assessment before certain out-of-Québec disclosures. | Determine applicability, identify the responsible privacy officer and publish accurate contact details where required; document purposes, means, categories, recipients, retention, rights, consent, safeguards and cross-border assessments. Review the regulator's guidance with counsel. |
| Canadian privacy | [OPC guidance](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-for-businesses/appropriate-handling-of-personal-information/gd_third-party_202609/) addresses third-party processing where PIPEDA applies. Free access alone does not decide whether the activity is commercial. | Counsel should determine the applicable federal/provincial regimes and document vendor, consent, access, breach and retention decisions. |
| French-language obligations | Québec explains that French-language rules apply to commercial publications, including qualifying websites and social posts. [Government guidance](https://www.quebec.ca/nouvelles/actualites/details/infraction-a-la-charte-de-la-langue-francaise-les-medias-sociaux-de-commandite-panache-inc-61693). | Establish whether the operator and this website are subject to those obligations and arrange an appropriate French version if required. Do not represent the current English-only site as legally reviewed. |
| Accessibility | WCAG 2.2 is used as a target, not a claim of certification. | Determine applicable obligations and complete real-device, screen-reader and keyboard audits. |

## Required human decisions before declaring the audit complete

1. Identify the operator and responsible privacy contact; confirm contact channels and provider retention practices.
2. Review and resolve the university name/domain/branding issue and obtain necessary permissions.
3. Perform a per-item rights assessment of all deployed assets, the CDN, past questions, music, fonts and third-party models; keep permissions and license evidence.
4. Review an accurate full privacy policy, terms, incident procedure and French-language position with Québec counsel. Never invent a retention period, permission, consent basis or compliance claim.

## External media and legal limits

YouTube lessons are click-to-load and use the privacy-enhanced embed host in the course player; this does not mean Google receives no data when the video is played. The [YouTube embed guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/171780) describes privacy-enhanced mode and applicable player terms. Verify embedding permissions, content availability, captions and required attribution individually. Optional outside videos remain available for INDU 211.
