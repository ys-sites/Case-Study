# Case Competition Evaluation Rubric & Scoring Criteria

This rubric reflects the unified judging standards of top-tier collegiate, national, and MBA case competitions. Use this matrix to audit case deliverables, pitch presentations, and simulation outputs before final evaluation.

---

## 1. Weighted Evaluation Categories

| Dimension | Weight | Target Criteria | High-Score Markers | Common Pitfalls & Point Deductions |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Strategic Insight & Diagnosis** | **25%** | Problem identification, root-cause depth, MECE issue tree logic, and strategic vision. | Clearly isolates the underlying economic bottleneck; avoids treating symptoms; presents an innovative yet credible strategic thesis. | Surface-level repetition of case text; descriptive rather than diagnostic; unstructured idea listing. |
| **2. Financial Viability & Modeling** | **25%** | Realistic proforma P&L, CapEx/OpEx allocation, unit economics (LTV/CAC), breakeven, and sensitivity. | 3-year proforma with explicit intermediate math; realistic margin progression; robust sensitivity matrix across 3 scenarios. | Arbitrary revenue spikes with no unit economic driver; math errors ($11+7 \ne 18$); omitting CapEx requirements. |
| **3. Implementation & Operations** | **20%** | Phased execution roadmap (Horizon 1–3), GTM plan, leading/lagging KPIs, and governance. | Specific milestone dates; realistic pilot phase; tangible sales incentive alignment; clear organizational ownership. | "We will launch nationwide in Month 1"; zero discussion of channel conflict or workforce capacity. |
| **4. Risk Management & Pre-Mortem** | **15%** | Identification of external/internal risks and actionable, concrete contingency mitigations. | 2x2 matrix with high-impact/high-probability risks pre-mitigated; defensible pre-mortem mitigations. | Generic risk list ("competitors might copy us"); no actionable contingency plan; ignoring regulatory barriers. |
| **5. Executive Presence & Q&A Defense** | **15%** | Deck typography, storytelling flow, action titles, time management, and Q&A resilience. | Slides tell a cohesive narrative via action titles alone; team answers adversarial judge questions with data and backup slides. | Wall of unreadable text; defensive or evasive during Q&A; running over the time limit. |

---

## 2. 100-Point Scoring Scale & Tier Definitions

- **90 – 100 (Podium / Winner Tier)**: Indistinguishable from McKinsey / BCG partner-level work. Deep analytical rigor, creative yet realistic strategy, air-tight financial proforma, and total Q&A composure.
- **80 – 89 (Finalist Tier)**: Solid strategic analysis, sound financial modeling with minor gaps in unit economics, structured implementation plan, and good presentation flow.
- **70 – 79 (Competitor Tier)**: Competent understanding of the case; identifiable strategy but lacks quantitative depth or relies on optimistic financial assumptions.
- **< 70 (Elimination Tier)**: Superficial diagnosis, critical arithmetic errors, generic recommendations, or failure to address core board mandates.
