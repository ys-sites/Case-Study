# 10-Slide Case Competition Presentation Deck

**Deck Title**: Operation Apex: Turning Commoditization into High-Margin Dominance  
**Team Designation**: Strategy Consulting Hub  

---

## Slide 1: Executive Summary & Recommendation
### Headline: Apex Can Double EBITDA to \$42.5M by Pivoting to Connected Telemetry Contracts
- **The Core Problem**: Unbundled aftermarket servicing allows third-party repair shops to capture 68% of recurring parts margins while Apex absorbs cyclical machinery sales slumps.
- **The Strategic Pivot**: Launch **Operation Apex**—reallocating \$14.2M into proprietary telemetry retrofits and guaranteed uptime SLAs.
- **Key Results**: Recurring revenue expands from 8.2% to **54.0%**, EBITDA reaches **\$42.5M**, and the investment yields a **28.4% IRR** with an **18-month payback**.

---

## Slide 2: Industry Dynamics & Root-Cause Diagnosis
### Headline: Margin Erosion is Driven by Lost Aftermarket Ownership, Not Manufacturing Quality
- **Surface Symptom**: Gross margins down -420 bps over 24 months.
- **Root Cause Analysis**: 4,200 installed units operate without telemetry connectivity. Customers purchase replacement bearings and seals from low-cost distributors because Apex lacks real-time diagnostic visibility.
- **Conclusion**: Selling hardware once is an obsolete industrial strategy; controlling the machine's operational data is the sole defensible moat.

---

## Slide 3: Market Opportunity & Addressable Sizing
### Headline: A \$185M High-Margin Brownfield Opportunity Exists Within Apex's Current Client Base
- **Total Addressable Market (TAM)**: \$8.4B global smart industrial automation space.
- **Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)**: \$2.1B North American automotive and consumer electronics brownfield plants.
- **Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)**: \$185M obtainable recurring telemetry contracts across Apex’s 380 existing enterprise accounts.

---

## Slide 4: Strategic Alternatives Considered
### Headline: Matching Low-Cost Clones on Price Destroys Profitability; Software-Only Pure Play Leaves Hardware Vulnerable
- **Alternative A (Price War)**: Match overseas competitors on machinery list prices.  
  *Outcome*: **Rejected**. Destroys \$18M in gross profit without creating customer switching costs.
- **Alternative B (Connected Telemetry EaaS)**: The recommended strategy. Bundles hardware with predictive SLAs.  
  *Outcome*: **Selected**. Drives LTV/CAC to 13.9x and produces 54% gross margins.
- **Alternative C (Overseas Expansion)**: Expand immediately into Asian markets.  
  *Outcome*: **Deferred**. Requires \$30M+ in localized sales channels with zero initial brand equity.

---

## Slide 5: The Recommended Strategy (3 Strategic Pillars)
### Headline: Operation Apex Executes Three Coordinated Pillars: Connect, Monetize, and Scale
- **Pillar 1: Connect (Months 1–6)**: Retrofit installed base with plug-and-play IoT gateway sensors.
- **Pillar 2: Monetize (Months 7–18)**: Shift sales model from spot billing to 3-year tiered uptime contracts (\$1,200 to \$2,800/mo).
- **Pillar 3: Scale (Months 19–36)**: Align regional distributor networks by granting an 18% recurring margin share on activated contracts.

---

## Slide 6: Go-to-Market & Operations Plan
### Headline: A Phased GTM Mitigates Operational Risk While Aligning Distributor Incentives
- **Target Buyer Persona**: VP of Manufacturing / Plant Operations Manager whose bonus depends on avoiding unplanned line shutdowns.
- **Pilot Phase (Q1–Q2)**: Onboard 20 enterprise clients (100 cells) with a "Guaranteed 99.5% Uptime" SLA rebate.
- **Sales Incentive Realignment**: Transition sales reps from 100% upfront commission to an ongoing 15% annuity trailing bonus on ARR renewals.
