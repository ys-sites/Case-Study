# Executive Briefing & Strategic Turnaround Recommendation

**Case Simulation**: NextGen Automation Turnaround  
**Target Organization**: Apex Industrial Automation Corp.  
**Engagement Horizon**: 3 Years (2026 – 2029)  
**Lead Entity**: Strategy Consulting Hub  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & The Core Thesis
Apex Industrial Automation faces severe operating margin compression (declining from 15.4% to 11.2% over 24 months) not due to aggregate market contraction, but as a direct consequence of **post-sale value capture leakage**. While Apex continues to manufacture high-reliability industrial robotic cells, 68% of recurring parts, preventative telemetry, and maintenance revenues are captured by uncertified third-party repair shops and commoditized clone suppliers.

### The Recommendation: Operation Apex
We recommend a decisive operational pivot from one-off transactional machinery sales to an integrated **Equipment-as-a-Service (EaaS) + Telemetry Maintenance SLA** model. By investing **\$14.2M** in proprietary IoT retrofit gateways and cloud diagnostic infrastructure, Apex will:
1. Elevate recurring revenue from 8.2% to **54.0%** of total turnover by Year 3.
2. Expand gross margins by **+1,150 basis points** (from 42.1% to 53.6%).
3. Deliver **\$42.5M in EBITDA** by Year 3 (up from \$12.5M), achieving an **IRR of 28.4%** with an **18.4-month capital payback**.
